# AI rules for laboratory reports
- This is a per-experiment project using the `persian-latex-kit` Git submodule.
- Read the laboratory manual and distinguish required simulation, theory, and optional physical assembly.
- Do not claim measurements or physical assembly happened unless actual evidence is supplied.
- Keep all personal data, circuit-specific answers, and photos out of the public kit repository.
- Use `\LabFigure` and `LabTable` for numbered captions and `\ref` for references.
- Compile with XeLaTeX after installing Vazirmatn. Preserve the source font requirements.
- In the prelab `\LabFinalfalse`; switch to `\LabFinaltrue` only after editing the results and final conclusion.
